R and J Transport Ltd is based in Sinfin, Derby, and has been in business for more than five years.

We operate a small, accountable fleet of 5+ rigid tippers and Class 1 box vehicles. Our drivers are Class 1 trained, and we provide haulage and transport solutions across the UK.

Our credentials are straightforward: FORS Silver accreditation and an Upper Tier Waste Carrier, Broker and Dealer registration with the Environment Agency.

FORS SILVER

What it means.

FORS is the Fleet Operator Recognition Scheme. Silver builds on Bronze with further requirements for safety, efficiency and environmental performance.

It includes monitoring fuel use, emissions and collisions, additional vehicle safety equipment and ongoing driver and manager training.
